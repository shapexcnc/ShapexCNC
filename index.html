// 01 — ABOUT US

WHO WE ARE

ShapeX CNC is a dedicated CNC routing workshop based in Isanpur, Ahmedabad. We bring your ideas to life with industrial precision — from intricate decorative panels to structural furniture components.

Whether you need a single custom prototype or a high-volume production run, our machines deliver clean, repeatable cuts every single time. We work with Wood, MDF, and PVC Board materials to suit any project requirement.

Our file-to-fabrication workflow is fast, accurate, and built around your deadlines. Send us your DXF, EPS, or CDR file and we'll take it from there.
